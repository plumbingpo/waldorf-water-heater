Code & Inspections

Safe Installations, Documented Service

Every commercial job we take on starts with safety. We inspect flue draft and vent connections, verify adequate combustion air, test relief valves and their discharge lines, confirm expansion tanks are charged correctly, and adjust mixing valves so water stored at higher temperatures leaves the tap at a safe temperature. We pull permits and schedule inspections where required. Findings go into a written report that ranks each issue by priority and recommends a specific fix.

This work serves a dual purpose: it protects people and property, and it provides the documentation that demonstrates due diligence to landlords, insurers, and inspectors. We maintain detailed service logs, temperature records, relief valve test results, and a documentation binder for every account, with more frequent service for heavy-use equipment in kitchens, laundries, and large mechanical rooms.

After-Hours Service

Rapid-Response Water Heater Repair for Businesses

A hot water failure on a commercial property can shut down a kitchen, cancel a day of salon appointments, leave hotel guests with cold showers, and expose the owner to tenant disputes. Waldorf Water Heater provides fast emergency water heater service for commercial properties throughout Waldorf and surrounding areas, and we schedule planned work after hours or before opening so your doors stay open.

No two commercial emergencies look the same. The cause could be a failed gas valve, a burned-out element, a flame sensor that stopped proving flame, a tripped high-limit switch, a leaking tank, a stalled recirculation pump, or a tankless unit throwing error codes. Whatever the cause, we diagnose it fast, restore hot water, and document the entire call for your facility files. Afterward, we put maintenance in place to keep it from happening again.
