How Bowie Homeowners Can Extend Water Heater Life

Many early failures start with maintenance that never happened, and the habits that prevent them are easy. Flush the tank every year to clear sediment, get the anode rod checked every few years, and test the relief valve annually so it doesn't seize. Keep the temperature near 120 degrees for safer water and less scale. For a tankless heater in a hard-water area, plan on descaling once a year so the heat exchanger stays clean.

Age matters too. Most tank water heaters run 8 to 12 years, tankless units often reach 20 with regular descaling, and heat pump models typically last 10 to 15. If your heater is past that window and showing symptoms like discolored hot water, popping and rumbling, moisture around the base, or showers that go cold sooner, a planned replacement is almost always cheaper and much easier on you than an emergency one after the tank lets go.

The earlier you report a hot water problem, the cheaper it usually is. A tank that's just begun to rumble needs only a flush, yet the same sediment, ignored for a few years, can create hot spots on the tank floor and shorten the life of the tank, forcing a replacement much sooner. Save our number in your contacts now so you can reach us fast the moment something sounds or looks wrong. A quick call saves time, money, and ruined floors.
